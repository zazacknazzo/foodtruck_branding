# Food Truck Federal

## Product and operation

Food Truck Federal is an independent business with approximately 30 years of activity in front of IFSP — Campus São Paulo. It has no institutional affiliation with IFSP. The mobile experience is the priority.

The goals are to reduce queues around 12h and 18h, increase sales through advertising and advance orders, and give the owner a simple operational interface. The business accepts Pix, cards and cash in person. These existing in-person payment methods are not currently available online features.

The owner agreed to digitalization. One person currently operates the truck; production capacity, preparation times and the preferred order-management interface are still being investigated. Do not invent these values or make collection-time promises.

Planned capabilities include a photographic catalog, categories, variants and extras, cart, online payments, immediate and scheduled collection subject to capacity limits, order tracking, administrative operation and campaign QR codes with origin tracking. They are not implemented by FTF-BOOT-001.
